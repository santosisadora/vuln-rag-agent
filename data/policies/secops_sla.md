# Corporate Remediation Policy (SecOps Standard v4.2)

- **Critical Severity (CVSS 9.0 - 10.0):** Mandatory patch within 48 hours. Generate Sev-1 ticket.
- **High Severity (CVSS 7.0 - 8.9):** Remediation required within 14 calendar days.
- **Medium Severity (CVSS 4.0 - 6.9):** Remediation scheduled within 30 calendar days.
- **Low Severity (CVSS 0.1 - 3.9):** Remediation within 90 calendar days.
